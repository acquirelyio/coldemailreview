Frequently asked questions

Which cold email tool is cheapest?

Acquirely starts lowest at $29/mo with sending included. Smartlead starts at $39/mo and Instantly at $47/mo, but both charge separately for lead data on their entry plans.

Which one should I pick for high volume?

Smartlead. Its $94 Pro plan includes 90,000 sends a month, and its $174 Smart plan includes 150,000 sends plus 50,000 verified emails.

Which has the biggest lead database?

Instantly, with a 450M+ lead database. It's billed as a separate credits plan from $47/mo, or bundled with sending from $94/mo.
