About Cold Email Review

Reviews of cold email software for founders, agencies and sales teams who want straight answers.

Who owns this site

Cold Email Review is owned by Acquirely, which makes cold email software and runs a cold email agency. Acquirely is one of the tools we review. We say so at the top of every article where it appears, and we list its weaknesses alongside its strengths.

How we write reviews

  • Every claim about a vendor links to that vendor's own pricing page or documentation, so you can check it.
  • Prices are list prices on the date shown on each article. Vendors change them often, so always confirm on their site before you buy.
  • Each tool gets strengths and weaknesses. No tool is best for everyone.
  • We don't take payment from other vendors for placement or ratings.

Spotted a mistake?

If a price or feature has changed, tell us through acquirely.io and we'll correct it.
