How we write reviews

1Source every claim

Every claim about a vendor links to that vendor's own pricing page or documentation, so you can check it.

2Date every price

Prices are list prices on the date shown on each article. Vendors change them often, so always confirm on their site before you buy.

3List the weaknesses

Each tool gets strengths and weaknesses. No tool is best for everyone.

4No paid placement

We don't take payment from other vendors for placement or ratings.

Spotted a mistake?

If a price or feature has changed, let us know and we'll correct it.
