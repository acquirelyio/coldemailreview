FAQ

Common questions

Short answers from the vendors' own pricing pages. Still unsure? Take the finder.

Which cold email tool is cheapest to start?

Of the three we compare, Acquirely has the lowest starting price at $29 a month, then Smartlead at $39 and Instantly at $47. See the price table.

Which tool has the biggest lead database?

Instantly lists a database of 450M+ B2B leads, sold as a separate credits plan from $47 a month (pricing).

Which tool is best for high sending volume?

Smartlead gives the most sends per dollar of the three: 90,000 sends a month on its $94 Pro plan (pricing).

Can I buy my own mailboxes and servers?

Smartlead sells Google, Outlook and SMTP mailboxes from about $4.50 a mailbox a month, and dedicated servers from $39 a server a month (pricing). Acquirely doesn't list dedicated servers.

Who owns Cold Email Review?

Acquirely, one of the tools we review. We say so wherever it appears, list its weaknesses like everyone else's, and link every price to its source. More about us.
