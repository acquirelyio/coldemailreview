Buying guide

The 5 types of cold email tool

Most tools fall into one of these groups. Pick the group first, then the tool.

1

Cold email platforms

Built to send from many inboxes at once, with warm-up, inbox rotation and a shared reply inbox. Best when cold email is your main channel and you send thousands a month.

See 13 tools →
2

Email + LinkedIn

One sequence that mixes emails with LinkedIn visits, invites and messages. Best when your buyers live on LinkedIn and you send lower volume.

See 5 tools →
3

Lead data + sending

A contact database first, with sequences on top. Best when finding the right people is the hard part, and you don't want to stitch two tools together.

See 4 tools →
4

Gmail and Outlook add-ons

Mail merge and tracking inside the inbox you already use. Cheapest way to start, but built for one inbox, not dozens.

See 5 tools →
5

Sales suites and CRMs

Sequences sit inside a bigger sales platform with calling, pipeline and reporting. Usually sold per seat on a contract to sales teams.
